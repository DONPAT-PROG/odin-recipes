Classic Lasagna Recipes

A traditional lasagna combines layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese filling, and melted mozzarella.

Ingredients
For the Meat Sauce

  • 500 g (1 lb) ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 800 g (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

For the Cheese Filling

  • 450 g (16 oz) ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley (optional)

Other Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions

1. Prepare the Meat Sauce
  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  2. Sauté onion until soft, then add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add ground beef and cook until browned.
  4. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper.
  5. Simmer for 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
2. Prepare the Cheese Mixture
  1. In a bowl, combine ricotta, egg, Parmesan, and parsley.
  2. Mix until smooth.
3. Cook the Noodles
  1. Boil lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Drain and lay flat to prevent sticking.
4. Assemble the Lasagna
  1. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a 9×13-inch baking dish.
  2. Add a layer of noodles.
  3. Spread one-third of the ricotta mixture over the noodles.
  4. Add meat sauce and a sprinkle of mozzarella.
  5. Repeat the layers two more times.
  6. Finish with remaining mozzarella and a little Parmesan on top.
5. Bake
  1. Cover with foil and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 25 minutes.
  2. Remove foil and bake for another 20–25 minutes until golden and bubbling.
6. Rest and Serve
  1. Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing.
  2. Serve with garlic bread and a fresh salad.
